# Stage 17 PARP1 Replacement Exploratory Preregistration

PARP1 is a human DNA-repair enzyme selected before downloading any PARP1 source
data. It is a non-pathogen, non-kinase target with ordinary noncovalent inhibitor
complexes and 508 clustered DUD-E actives, exactly sufficient for the fixed 498
active-molecule panel requirement.

HIVPR and DPP4 remain in the provenance record. Neither branch completed target
benchmark docking or read enrichment outcomes. PARP1 is an exploratory extension,
not a replacement inside the original three-target confirmatory comparison.

The first PARP1 gate is intentionally inexpensive: verify that all 498 required
actives can be split into scaffold-disjoint Train-696, fresh-validation, and locked
test panels. Only then proceed to coordinate screening. Receptors will be selected
from 24 label-free structural candidates after the unchanged three-seed redocking
gate, with 16 final receptors required for production docking.
