# Stage 16 DPP4 Replacement Exploratory Preregistration

## Decision

HIVPR was declared externally unavailable after source audit and before any receptor
discovery, docking, or enrichment analysis. Its record is retained. DPP4 starts a
new preregistered exploratory extension and is not silently substituted into the
original three-target confirmatory gate.

## Why DPP4

DPP4 has 533 clustered DUD-E actives and 109 RCSB X-ray entries, enough for the
unchanged ligand-panel sizes and a broad receptor search. It is a non-kinase target
without a required catalytic-metal or covalent docking model.

## Technical Amendment

Twenty-four receptors will be selected using coordinates only and subjected to the
unchanged three-seed Uni-Dock cognate-redocking gate. If at least 16 pass, the final
16 will be selected deterministically by structural max-min diversity among passing
receptors. No activity labels or benchmark docking scores are allowed in either
selection step. Fewer than 16 passing candidates closes the DPP4 technical gate.

## Scientific Boundary

This prospective amendment improves technical feasibility without changing the
QUBO, comparators, panel sizes, or BEDROC endpoint. A positive DPP4 result remains
exploratory portability evidence and cannot erase prior target failures or establish
quantum advantage.
